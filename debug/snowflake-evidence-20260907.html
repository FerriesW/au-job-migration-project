What the three engines cost

EngineReportedBilling model
BigQuery622,713 bytesper byte scanned, 1 TiB/month free
Athena4,727,503 bytesper byte scanned, $5/TB, 10 MB minimum
Snowflake1.0137 creditsper credit-second of warehouse uptime

Athena reads 7.6x the bytes for identical answers - storage format, not workload: BigQuery reads only the columns a query names, Athena decompresses whole JSON objects. Snowflake is not on that axis at all. 1.01 credits covered the entire track - building the account, loading the data, every dbt run and every repeat of the equivalence checks - against a $400 trial allowance. Billing landed on only 6 days out of roughly three weeks, because AUTO_SUSPEND = 60 puts the warehouse to sleep a minute after the last query.
